School Timetable

Timetable chart: Class + Section on the left and Periods across the top. Select the subject and teacher inside each period cell. Teacher clashes for the same day and period are shown in red.

Each cell contains Subject and Teacher/Staff. Subjects come from the class subject allotment. The Teacher dropdown contains the full active staff list for every class and subject. Timetable is the primary assignment screen; saving it synchronizes Teacher & Subject Allocation and Staff Result permissions.
